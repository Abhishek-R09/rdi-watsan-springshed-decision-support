# Himmotthan spring-data request

## Purpose

We are building a transparent decision-support tool for spring vulnerability, provisional recharge-zone mapping and Direct Injection planning support for the SRHU/RDI WATSAN DoCC Project 1 team. We request only records that Himmotthan is willing and permitted to share for academic project use.

## Requested extract and documentation

For each available spring record, please provide the following where available:

- stable spring ID, locality/village/block/district, latitude and longitude, coordinate reference system and inventory date;
- spring type, current condition/status, source ownership/management and dependent households;
- discharge measurements by date and season, including method, units, replicate readings and rainfall conditions;
- water-quality measurements, laboratory/field method, date and units;
- geology, slope/structure observations and any hydrogeological notes;
- recharge-area polygon or area only with the delineation method, date and field-verification status;
- treatment/intervention details, dates and condition/maintenance records;
- groundwater-level, rainfall, spring-flow or yield monitoring time series, including missing-value codes and equipment/method;
- data dictionary, source/owner, licence or sharing terms, and known quality limitations.

## Important safeguards

We will preserve the extract as received, attribute Himmotthan as the source, and separate it from our own field data. We will not publish coordinates, community details, photos or monitoring records beyond the permission given. A reported change after a treatment will be labelled as an observed association unless its before/after design, conditions and comparison basis support a stronger claim.

## Priority geography

Tehri is the first priority because it overlaps the internship’s Chamba context. Records from Pithoragarh or other Himalayan sites remain valuable as reference evidence but will not be represented as site-specific Chamba or Satpuli data.
