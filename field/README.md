# Field-data intake

Create one dated folder per visit in `data/00_raw/field/YYYY-MM-DD_<site>/`. Keep photos and original device exports unchanged. A field record must identify:

- spring/DI site identifier and GPS coordinate;
- observer, date, time and weather/rainfall conditions;
- at least three bucket-and-stopwatch discharge readings with bucket volume and elapsed time;
- whether recent heavy rain may distort the reading;
- source type, seasonal-flow recall, dependent households, alternative sources and pollution risks;
- recharge-work condition and rock-tilt/structural observations where feasible;
- for DI: roof area, rainfall basis, filter and first-flush condition, handpump elevation/yield, distance to spring, water-level reading and water-quality results where available.

Label each statement as **observed**, **measurement**, **community recall**, **staff explanation**, or **project inference**. User-recalled changes are not DI impact measurements.
